# Holy Week Dallal content

Source: `incoming/hollyweak/`. The source ZIP was omitted because it duplicates the extracted files.

- `Dallal/books.csv`: two Arabic books, 1,046 ordered records: **هناك كنت معه** (أ. حلمي القمص يعقوب) and **يسوع المصلوب** (القس منسى يوحنا).
- `Dallal/contents.csv`: 209 content headings, including 34 absent from the earlier `copticprayer/Dlal` export.
- `Dallal/contents_relations.csv`: 385 links preserving content order, including 51 absent from the earlier export.
- `Dallal/sentences.csv`: 1,216 Holy Week text records.
- `Dallal/days.csv` and `Dallal/prays_relations.csv`: source relationship tables retained for a complete, usable package.
- `Dallal/INDEX.json`, `MASTER_INDEX.json`, and `README_AR.txt`: original source indexes and notes.
